## 4. Keyframe Narrative & Experiential Walkthrough

### D01 — ARRIVAL: The Spatial Monolith
* **Experience Moment:** First viewport encounter. The visitor encounters ALGORYXZ not as a conventional website header, but as a central spatial core suspended in an atmospheric dark void.
* **Spatial & 3D Composition:** A precision-machined kinetic monolith deriving from the angular geometry of "XZ". Dark basalt obsidian surfaces with hairline bronze/brass seams catching raking amber light.
* **Typographic Anchor:** High-contrast statement *"WE MAKE THINGS FOR THE INTERNET."* positioned with deliberate editorial weight. Tactile coordinate indicators imply physical camera perspective.

### D02 — DECONSTRUCTION: Monolith Unfolding
* **Experience Moment:** 50–70vh later. Complete physical and spatial continuity from D01.
* **Spatial Transformation:** The central monolith mechanically cleaves along its basalt strata into distinct architectural plates moving along the camera's Z-axis (`Z: +1.60`, `PARALLAX OCCLUSION ENGAGED`).
* **Typographic Response:** The primary headline breaks dynamically into dimensional strata: *"WE MAKE... ...FOR THE INTERNET."* Internal technical coordinates (`/000`, `/001`, `/002`) begin surfacing from within the inner chambers.

### D03 — ARCHIVE: Spatial Project Discovery
* **Experience Moment:** The hero object resolves into project discovery.
* **Spatial Composition:** Three floating architectural slabs suspended at varying depths and tilt angles (`TILT: +24°`, `FOCAL DEPTH: 100%`, `TILT: -15°`), strictly rejecting cookie-cutter card grids:
  - `Plate /000`: `ALGORYXZ / 000 — INTERNAL — Studio Engine`
  - `Plate /001` (Center Dominant): `ALGORYXZ / 001 — CONCEPT — Celebration & Living Memory Archive`
  - `Plate /002`: `ALGORYXZ / 002 — CONCEPT — Independent Business / Brand`
* **Interaction Cue:** `[CLICK TO ENTER WORLD /001]`.

### D04 — PROJECT FOCUS: Transitioning to 001
* **Experience Moment:** Viewer interacts with `ALGORYXZ / 001`.
* **Visual Dynamics:** Plate `/001` surges into the extreme foreground, tilting toward the camera lens. Its warm, emotional amber lighting expands and "contaminates" the dark Algoryxz chassis (`WORLD CONTAMINATION: 88%`). Surrounding plates blur into atmospheric background fog.
* **Viewer Sensation:** Clear optical indication: *"I am about to enter another world."*

### D05 — PROJECT WORLD: Inside Celebration 001
* **Experience Moment:** Full immersion completed. Algoryxz identity recedes to a discreet anchor (`← RETURN TO ALGORYXZ / ARCHIVE · CONCEPT PROJECT`).
* **Visual Atmosphere:** The celebration world owns the entire screen. Intimate candlelight chiaroscuro, spoken vow inscriptions (*"I promise to return to this sanctuary whenever the skies break..."*), sensory 432Hz master audio acoustic track waveform, and a terracotta wax seal guestbook ledger. Honest concept representation.

### D06 — TWO WORLDS: Spatial Dichotomy
* **Experience Moment:** Visualizing the studio's dual territory without two rectangular cards.
* **Spatial Composition:** A split-screen architectural threshold titled *"TWO WORLDS. ONE CRAFT."*:
  - **Left (World 01 — Commerce & Atelier):** Tactile basalt, architectural serif typography, designed for culinary roasteries, creators, and independent flagships.
  - **Right (World 02 — Living Digital Heirlooms):** Warm luminous linen, candlelight glow, designed for weddings, secret proposals, and family memoirs.
* **Interactive Seam:** A central perspective slider (`Shift Perspective: W01 [30%] ---o--- W02 [70%]`) allowing the visitor to dynamically tilt lighting and depth between both worlds.

### D07 — PROCESS OBJECT: The Kinetic Continuum
* **Experience Moment:** The 5-stage studio methodology (`DISCOVER`, `DESIGN`, `BUILD`, `LAUNCH`, `GROW`) visualized as **ONE continuous evolving spatial sculpture** titled *"A CONTINUOUS DISCIPLINE."*
* **Transformation Phases:**
  1. *01 Discover:* Raw unhewn basalt block with acoustic listening probes.
  2. *02 Design:* Faceted crystal with glowing wireframe blueprints.
  3. *03 Build:* Stacked obsidian plates, precision brass spacers, zero-bloat semantic structure.
  4. *04 Launch:* Pressurized kinetic valve emitting warm amber vapor.
  5. *05 Grow:* Spatial infinite lattice with self-optimizing telemetry.

### D08 — FOUNDERS / HUMANITY: Three Builders
* **Experience Moment:** A calm, authentic human transition after the spatial journey.
* **Truthful Studio Representation:**
  - *"Three People. No Middle Layer."*
  - **SMARAK** — *Engineering & Architecture*
  - **DEEPTIMAN** — *Creative Direction & Design*
  - **AKRITI** — *Operations, Delivery & QA*
* **Craft Manifesto:** *"Three young minds based in Odisha, India, making things for the internet. When you work with Algoryxz, you work directly with the people writing the code and drawing the vectors."*
* **Zero AI-avatar tropes:** Clean typography, warm paper/slate materiality, and a restrained invitation: `LET'S BUILD SOMETHING UNFORGETTABLE.` (`founders@algoryxz.com`).
